# Changelog

## v0.1.1-demo — Permanent CRT Presentation

Android version **0.1.1 (build 2)**.

- Keep the existing CRT presentation permanently active across menus, the story introduction, gameplay and its overlays, and the controller panel.
- Remove the CRT switch from Options and remove the saved on/off preference.
- Preserve the current CRT strengths, gameplay monitor appearance, controls, and gameplay balance.

The story introduction and existing gameplay systems continue unchanged. This remains a demo / work in progress.

## v0.1.0-demo — Android Demo

**Work in progress.** This early demo includes:

- Playable Kabu and Kapu with water- and fire-themed combat
- Endless survival runs with escalating enemy difficulty
- Automatic orbiting elemental attacks, active Dash, and projectile barrage
- XP gem and food-based health pickups
- Level-up choices with Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary rarities, including character-specific upgrades
- Preta, Poison Hag, Raven, and Pulgasari enemies
- A three-phase Zodiac Lamb boss encounter that repeats every five minutes
- Mobile joystick, **A**, **B**, and **START** controls
- Retro CRT / arcade visual presentation
- Current menu and gameplay 8-bit music credited to Megumi Ryu (YUKARINOTI), plus the boss theme by Yuki Nozawa

Features, balancing, visuals, audio, and content may change as development continues.
